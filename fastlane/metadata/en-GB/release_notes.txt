Version 1.16.0

[Improvements]
- Long recordings are now more reliable. If the app closes while recording, the recording is back in your list the next time you open it
- Recording now resumes automatically after interruptions such as phone calls. If it can't resume, you'll get a notification
- Reduced battery use while recording in the background
- Notification titles and preset tags now appear in your language

[Bug Fixes]
- Fixed recording sometimes staying stopped after connecting or disconnecting earphones or Bluetooth devices
